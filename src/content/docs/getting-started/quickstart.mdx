Follow these three steps to upload your first video cut and get timestamped feedback from your client.

---

## Step 1: Sign in and click "Upload Cut"

1. Open [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in) in your browser.
2. Sign in using your **Google account** or request an **Email Magic Link**.
3. Once in your Library, click the **Upload Cut** button in the top right.

:::tip[Resumable Uploads]
You can drag and drop your exported MP4 or MOV file directly into the upload dropzone. Uploads stream directly from your browser to our video network, keeping the process fast and stable.
:::

---

## Step 2: Copy your review link

1. As soon as the upload completes, your video is encoded for smooth web streaming.
2. In your library card or on the video player page, click **Share cut** or **Copy link**.
3. The review link is copied to your clipboard (it looks like `https://app.crewcut.in/review/v/...`).

:::note[Download Protection]
By default, clients can only watch and comment. If you want your client to be able to download the source file, toggle on **"Allow file download"** in the share modal.
:::

---

## Step 3: Send it to your client

1. Paste the review link directly into WhatsApp, Slack, or an email to your client.
2. Tell them: *"Here is the latest cut. You can watch and comment directly on the timeline—no account or login required."*
3. When they type feedback, you will see their notes organized neatly in your sidebar, tagged with exact seconds like `00:45` or `02:18`.

---
