**CrewCut** is a video review and collaboration workspace built for freelance video editors, motion designers, and creative studios.

If you edit video for clients, you know the headache: you send a Google Drive link or export to YouTube Unlisted, and your client sends back feedback like:
- *"Change the font at that part where the guy talks about money."*
- *"Can we cut the music right before the beat drops?"*
- Voice notes on WhatsApp with background noise.

You spend half your time trying to figure out which second they are talking about.

CrewCut solves this in three simple steps:

```
1. You upload your video cut  ──▶  2. You send a private link  ──▶  3. Client clicks & comments at the exact second
```

---

## Why Video Editors Love CrewCut

### 1. Zero Login for Clients
Your client does **not** need to make an account, remember a password, or download an app. They simply tap your link on their phone or laptop, watch the video, and leave notes.

### 2. Exact Timestamped Feedback
When your client types a comment, CrewCut automatically attaches the exact timecode (for example, `01:42`). Clicking that timecode jumps your player right to that frame.

### 3. Direct Resumable Uploads
We use direct chunk-based uploads (powered by Bunny Stream). If your internet flickers or your mobile hotspot drops, your upload doesn't start over from 0%—it picks up right where it left off.

### 4. Transparent Quotas in INR
Pricing is straightforward, priced in Indian Rupees (INR), and designed to protect you from surprise cloud bandwidth bills.

## The Two Main Roles

| Role | Account Required? | What They Can Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Creator / Editor (Owner)** | **Yes** (Sign in with Google or Email link) | Upload videos, organize folders, share review links, manage comments, track storage and billing. |
| **Reviewer / Client (Guest)** | **No** (Zero signup needed) | Watch the cut, scrub the timeline, leave timestamped comments, reply to comment threads. |
