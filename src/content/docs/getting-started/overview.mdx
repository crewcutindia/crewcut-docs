**CrewCut** is a video review and creative collaboration workspace built for freelance video editors, motion designers, directors, and boutique agencies.

If you edit video for clients, you know the traditional headache: you upload to Google Drive or YouTube Unlisted, and your client replies on WhatsApp with vague notes like:
- *"Change the font at that part where the speaker talks about money."*
- *"Can we cut the music right before the beat drops?"*
- Fragmented, noisy audio voice clips without frame references.

CrewCut eliminates revision confusion in three simple steps:

```
1. Upload your cut & assets  ──▶  2. Share a zero-login link  ──▶  3. Client leaves frame notes & voice clips
```

---

## Why Creative Teams Choose CrewCut

### 1. Zero Login for Clients
Your clients and stakeholders do **not** need to create an account, remember a password, or download software. They simply tap your link on their phone or desktop, watch the cut, and leave feedback.

### 2. Frame-Accurate Notes & 100s Voice Notes
Clients can type notes at the exact frame or record up to **100 seconds of audio voice feedback** with live waveform visualization, making revision intent crystal-clear.

### 3. Visual Frame Drawing & Annotations
Reviewers can draw arrows, sketch boxes, and circle elements directly on the video canvas to point out exact visual adjustments.

### 4. 9:16 Vertical Reels & Shorts Player
Dedicated vertical player layout engineered specifically for mobile-first Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok cuts.

### 5. CrewCut Drive (Companion Assets)
Host project files (`.prproj`, `.aep`), master audio stems (`.wav`), Photoshop thumbnails (`.psd`), and briefs in a unified folder alongside your video cuts.

### 6. Adobe Premiere Pro & After Effects Extension
Sync client review comments directly into your Premiere Pro or After Effects timeline as native sequence markers with a single click.

### 7. Direct Streaming Downloads
Fast, native browser downloads for original master cuts and 720p/1080p proxies with zero memory buffering bottlenecks.

## The Two Core Roles

| Role | Account Required? | What They Can Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Creator / Editor (Owner)** | **Yes** (Sign in via OTP or Google) | Upload cuts, organize folders, share review links, manage companion assets, track quotas, and sync to Adobe Premiere Pro/AE. |
| **Reviewer / Client (Guest)** | **No** (Zero signup needed) | Stream cuts, scrub the timeline, leave frame-accurate comments, record voice notes, and download approved cuts (if permitted). |
