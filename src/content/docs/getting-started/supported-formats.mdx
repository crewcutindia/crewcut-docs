CrewCut accepts standard video formats exported from Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, and After Effects, as well as companion production files.

---

## 1. Supported Video Formats & Codecs

| Container Format | File Extension | Transcoding Status | Recommended Export Settings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MP4** | `.mp4` | **Best (Recommended)** | H.264 or H.265 (HEVC), AAC Audio |
| **QuickTime** | `.mov` | Supported | Apple ProRes 422, H.264, or Animation |
| **WebM** | `.webm` | Supported | VP9 or AV1, Opus audio |
| **MKV / TS** | `.mkv`, `.ts` | Supported | Standard web video streams |

---

## 2. High Dynamic Range & Resolutions

- **Resolutions:** From standard 720p / 1080p Full HD up to **4K UHD** and **8K Ultra HD** playback.
- **HDR & Dolby Vision:** Full color-accurate HDR and Dolby Vision playback preservation when uploaded from supported NLE workflows.
- **Aspect Ratios:** Standard 16:9 widescreen, 1:1 square, and **9:16 vertical** (automatically triggers vertical Reels review layout on mobile and desktop).

---

## 3. Supported Companion Assets (CrewCut Drive)

You can upload non-video production assets directly into your project folders:

- **Audio Stems:** `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.aac`, `.flac`, `.aiff`
- **Project Archives:** `.prproj` (Premiere Pro), `.aep` (After Effects)
- **Design & Thumbnails:** `.psd` (Photoshop), `.png`, `.jpg`, `.svg`, `.webp`
- **Documents & Briefs:** `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`
- **Typography:** `.otf`, `.ttf`, `.woff2`

---

## 4. Export Presets for Rapid Uploads

For quick client review cycles, fast uploads save time:
1. **Resolution:** 1080p is optimal for daily review cuts. 4K is ideal for final color and master sign-offs.
2. **Codec:** **H.264** delivers the fastest browser upload and instantaneous cloud transcoding.
3. **Bitrate:**
   - 1080p: **10–15 Mbps** (VBR 1-pass).
   - 4K: **25–40 Mbps**.
4. **DaVinci & Premiere Presets:** The **"YouTube 1080p"** or **"Vimeo 1080p"** presets offer the best balance of visual fidelity and compact file size.

---

## 5. File Size Limits by Tier

- **Free Tier:** Up to available account storage (5 GB total account quota).
- **Starter, Plus, & Studio Tiers:** Single file uploads up to **10 GB** per master cut.
- **Contributor Review Uploads:** Contributor uploads through shared folder links are capped at **1 GB** per file to protect the folder owner's storage headroom.
