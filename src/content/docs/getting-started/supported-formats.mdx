CrewCut accepts almost any video format exported from popular editing suites like Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, and After Effects.

---

## Supported File Containers

| Container Format | File Extension | Status | Recommended Settings |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MP4** | `.mp4` | **Best (Recommended)** | H.264 or H.265 (HEVC), AAC Audio |
| **QuickTime** | `.mov` | Supported | ProRes 422, H.264, or Animation codec |
| **WebM** | `.webm` | Supported | VP9 or AV1, Opus audio |
| **MPEG-TS / MKV** | `.mkv`, `.ts` | Supported | Web-standard codecs |

---

## Export Recommendations for Fastest Uploads

When you are sending a draft cut for client review, speed is key:

1. **Resolution**: 1080p (Full HD) is standard for quick reviews and uploads smoothly on mobile networks. 4K is also supported if your plan has enough storage.
2. **Codec**: **H.264** provides the fastest browser upload and instant web transcoding.
3. **Bitrate**: 
   - 1080p: Target **10–15 Mbps** (VBR 1-pass).
   - 4K: Target **25–40 Mbps**.
4. **Audio**: Stereo AAC at **192 kbps** or **320 kbps**, 48 kHz.

:::tip[Export Presets in DaVinci & Premiere]
In DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, selecting the **"YouTube 1080p"** or **"Vimeo 1080p"** export preset creates the sweet spot between crisp image quality and compact file size.
:::

---

## File Size Limits

- **Free Tier**: Up to the remaining free storage in your account (2 GB total account cap).
- **Starter, Plus, and Studio**: Single files up to **10 GB** per cut.
- **Contributor Uploads**: Shared folder uploads from guests are capped at **500 MB** per file to prevent quota exhaustion.

---

## What Happens After You Upload?

Once your file finishes uploading, our cloud video pipeline (Bunny Stream) encodes your video into adaptive bitrate streaming (HLS). This means:
- Clients on fast Wi-Fi automatically get crisp, high-definition playback.
- Clients on mobile data stream smoothly without stuttering or buffering.
