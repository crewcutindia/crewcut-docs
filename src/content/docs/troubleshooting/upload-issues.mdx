If your video upload seems stuck or is taking longer than expected, here is how to resolve it quickly.

---

## 1. What to Do if an Upload Pauses

Because CrewCut uses the **TUS resumable upload protocol**, temporary network drops do not destroy your upload progress.

- **Check Your Internet Connection**: If you are on Wi-Fi or a mobile hotspot, confirm your connection is active.
- **Keep the Tab Open**: Do not close or hard-refresh the tab while the progress bar shows an active upload. If the connection reconnects within a few minutes, the upload automatically continues.
- **If It Gets Stuck for Over 5 Minutes**: Refresh the page, select the exact same file again, and click upload. The uploader checks the server chunk cache and resumes from where it stopped.

---

## 2. Speeding Up Slow Uploads

If uploading a 2 GB file is taking too long:
1. **Switch Codec to H.264**: ProRes or uncompressed QuickTime files are enormous (often 10–20 GB for a short video). Exporting an H.264 draft cut at 12–15 Mbps reduces file size by 80% with virtually no visible difference on client screens.
2. **Disable Heavy VPNs**: Some VPNs throttle large video transfers. Try pausing your VPN during the upload.
3. **Use Google Chrome or Brave**: Modern Chromium browsers have the most efficient stream chunk buffers for large file transfers.

---

## 3. "Storage Quota Exceeded" Error

If an upload fails with a quota alert:
- Check your remaining account storage on your dashboard.
- Remember the **$\sim 1.5\times$ Bunny encoding rule**: if your file is 1 GB, your account needs $\sim 1.5$ GB of available room for the transcoded web renditions.
- Delete older completed cuts or [upgrade your plan](/plans-invoices/pricing-plans/) to increase your storage.
