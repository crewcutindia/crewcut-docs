If your video upload seems stuck or a video download prompt doesn't appear, here is how to resolve it quickly.

---

## 1. What to Do if an Upload Pauses

CrewCut uses resumable chunk uploads directly to our edge network:
- **Keep the Tab Open:** If your Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot drops temporarily, do not close the tab. Once connection returns, the upload automatically continues.
- **Resuming a Stalled Upload:** If an upload freezes for over 3 minutes, refresh the page and select the exact same file again. The uploader verifies existing chunks and resumes from where it left off.
- **Export Tips:** Exporting an H.264 MP4 cut at 12–15 Mbps reduces file size by 80% compared to ProRes, saving upload time with zero perceptible quality difference for client review.

---

## 2. Resolving Download Prompts & Issues

When you or a reviewer clicks **Download Video**:
- **Save File Dialog:** The streaming Service Worker prompts your native browser download manager immediately. If no download begins, check whether your browser is blocking automatic download popups for `app.crewcut.in`. Click the permissions icon in your URL bar and select **Allow automatic downloads**.
- **Rate Limit Alerts:** If you trigger multiple rapid downloads in succession, you may receive a rate-limit notice (5 downloads per minute). Wait 60 seconds before initiating the next download.
- **Permissions Disabled:** If the download button is missing on a review link, the creator has disabled file downloads in the review link settings. Contact the video owner to enable download permissions.

---

## 3. Storage Quota Exceeded

If an upload fails with a storage quota notification:
- Check your remaining account storage on your dashboard.
- Delete completed cuts or obsolete companion assets to free up space.
- Alternatively, activate an instant [Storage Add-on Pack](/storage-bandwidth/storage-addons/) (+10 GB, +25 GB, +30 GB, or +50 GB) for immediate headroom.
