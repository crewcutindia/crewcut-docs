## Adaptive Resolution Switching

CrewCut players use **HLS (HTTP Live Streaming)**:
- **Auto Quality**: The player automatically detects the viewer's network speed. If they are on a high-speed fiber connection, it serves 1080p or 4K. If they are on mobile data on a train, it drops smoothly to 720p or 480p to prevent pauses.
- **Manual Resolution Switcher**: Viewers can click the **Gear (Settings)** icon in the video player to lock the resolution to 1080p, 720p, or 360p.

---

## Why is a Brand New Video Showing "Processing"?

Immediately after an upload finishes:
1. The video enters the cloud encoding queue.
2. High-definition resolutions and timeline scrubbing preview thumbnails are generated.
3. This usually takes between **30 seconds to 2 minutes** depending on the length of the cut.
4. Once processing finishes, the status updates to **Ready** and the review link becomes active.
