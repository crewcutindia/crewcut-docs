When working on unreleased commercial ads, confidential films, or sensitive client footage, security is critical.

Here is how CrewCut safeguards your video media and data.

---

## 1. Token-Authenticated Video Delivery

Your video files are never exposed via public static URLs. 
- Every video stream is guarded by **Bunny Token Authentication** with SHA256 hashed signatures.
- Tokens expire on-demand and are tied to your specific review session.
- Third parties cannot scrape, leech, or hotlink your video stream into external players.

---

## 2. Review Links Are Secret URLs

Review links use cryptographically random high-entropy identifiers (e.g. `/review/v/7f9a2b8e...`):
- They are **unlisted**: search engines like Google and Bing are instructed never to index review pages.
- Only people you give the link to can view the cut.
- If you ever need to revoke access, simply delete the video from your library. The link goes dead instantly.

---

## 3. Strict Download Controls

- Reviewer downloads are **disabled by default**.
- Unless you specifically toggle on **"Allow file download"** in the share modal, there is no download button for viewers.

---

## 4. Account & Payment Data Security

- **Database & Auth**: Hosted on Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled across all tables, ensuring only authenticated video owners can modify their assets.
- **Payment Security**: Razorpay handles all card, UPI, and bank transactions with RBI-mandated PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance. CrewCut never sees or stores your credit card numbers or UPI PINs.
