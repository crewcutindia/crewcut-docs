When handling unreleased commercial cuts, confidential feature films, or sensitive corporate video assets, privacy and protection are non-negotiable.

Here is how CrewCut secures your creative media and data.

---

## 1. Token-Authenticated Video Streaming

Your video assets are never exposed via unauthenticated public URLs:
- **Expiring HMAC-SHA256 Signatures:** Every video stream and download request is protected by cryptographic tokens signed with time-limited expiration.
- **Hotlink & Leech Defense:** External scrapers or unauthorized third parties cannot hotlink or rip your video streams.

---

## 2. Password Protection & Link Expiry

In addition to unlisted, high-entropy review URLs:
- **Passcode Protection:** You can set a private passcode on any video or folder review link. Viewers must enter the passcode before the player decrypts and streams.
- **Expiry Dates:** Configure review links to automatically self-destruct after a specified date.
- **Instant Revocation:** Toggling off a review link immediately invalidates access for all external viewers.

---

## 3. Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP 2FA)

Protect your creator account against credential compromise:
1. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Security** in [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Enable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**.
3. Scan the QR code using Google Authenticator, 1Password, or Authy.
4. Save your emergency backup recovery codes.

---

## 4. Microphone Privacy & Voice Notes

- Microphones are accessed **only** when you or a reviewer explicitly click the microphone icon to record a voice note.
- Browsers enforce visual recording indicators (orange/red dot in address bar).
- Audio recordings are processed securely and deleted automatically when the parent comment or video cut is removed.

---
