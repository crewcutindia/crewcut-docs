Uploading gigabytes of video footage on Indian home broadband or 4G/5G mobile hotspots used to be risky—one small network hitch and the upload failed.

CrewCut uses **direct resumable chunk-based uploads** (the open **TUS** protocol). Here is how it works and how to get the most out of it.

---

## How Direct Resumable Upload Works

When you drag a video into CrewCut:
1. **Direct Connection**: Your file streams directly from your web browser to our CDN video servers (Bunny Stream). It never routes through intermediary servers, which maximizes your upload speed.
2. **Chunked Transfers**: The video is broken down into small, verified data chunks.
3. **Automatic Resume**: If your Wi-Fi flickers, your phone switches towers, or your laptop sleeps, the upload automatically pauses. When the connection returns, it **resumes from the last saved chunk** instead of starting over.

:::tip[Keep the Tab Open]
While uploads are resilient to brief disconnects, you should keep the browser tab open until the progress bar reaches 100% and displays **"Processing completed"**.
:::

---

## Step-by-Step: Uploading a Cut

1. From your **Library**, click the **Upload Cut** button.
2. Either drag your video file into the box or click **Browse Files**.
3. Choose the target folder (or leave it in your root library).
4. Watch the progress bar show current upload speed and percentage.
5. Once uploaded, transcoding begins automatically. You will see a thumbnail appear as soon as the cut is ready to watch.

---

## Replacing Revised Cuts (v1 Workflow)

When your client gives feedback and you render an updated draft:
- In CrewCut v1, simply upload the new cut as a fresh video (e.g., `BrandFilm_v2.mp4`).
- Group both cuts in the same client folder to keep your project organized.
- Copy and share the new link with your client.

:::note[Why No Version Stacks?]
To keep CrewCut fast and free of clutter, v1 treats each video file cleanly on its own. You can delete older draft cuts whenever you need to free up storage space.
:::
