Video editing workflows rarely consist of just an MP4 cut. You often need to exchange master audio stems, Photoshop thumbnails, project files, and client briefs. **CrewCut Drive** allows you to host all companion assets directly alongside your video review links.

---

## 1. Supported Companion File Formats

CrewCut supports all standard creative media formats:

| Category | Supported File Formats |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Audio Stems** | `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.aac`, `.flac`, `.aiff` |
| **Project Files** | `.prproj` (Premiere Pro), `.aep` (After Effects) |
| **Graphics & Thumbnails** | `.psd` (Photoshop), `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.svg`, `.webp` |
| **Documents & Briefs** | `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt` |
| **Typography & Fonts** | `.otf`, `.ttf`, `.woff2` |

---

## 2. Uploading Companion Assets

1. Navigate to your project folder or open a video details view in [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Switch to the **Companion Assets** tab.
3. Drag and drop your files or click **Upload Assets**.
4. CrewCut verifies file checksums and securely uploads your assets directly to our cloud storage network.

---

## 3. Storage Accounting

All companion assets consume your account's unified cloud storage quota:

- **Unified Quota:** Your total storage bar displays both video files (and their adaptive HLS streaming renditions) and companion assets in a single consolidated meter.
- **Quota Headroom:** If an asset upload would exceed your tier limit, CrewCut alerts you before processing, allowing you to free up space or activate an instant [Storage Add-on Pack](/storage-bandwidth/storage-addons).

---

## 4. Sharing Assets with Clients & Collaborators

You can control whether companion assets are visible or downloadable on shared review links:

- **Review Link Visibility:** In the review link settings modal, toggle **"Include Companion Assets"** on or off.
- **Client Access:** When enabled, clients and sound engineers viewing the review link see an **Assets** tab alongside the video player, allowing them to preview graphics and download production files directly without needing a CrewCut login.
