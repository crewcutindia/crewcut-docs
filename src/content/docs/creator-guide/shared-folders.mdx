Sometimes a client project involves multiple cuts—like 10 short reels, 3 interview drafts, or different social aspect ratios (9:16 and 16:9). 

Instead of sending 10 different links, you can share a single **Folder Link**.

---

## 1. Sharing a Folder Playlist

1. Open your Library and locate your client folder.
2. Click the **three dots (⋮)** menu on the folder card and choose **Share Folder**.
3. Copy the public folder link.
4. When your client opens this link, they see a clean playlist of all video cuts in that folder. They can click any cut to watch and leave timestamped feedback.

:::note[Anonymous Playlist Viewing]
Just like individual review cuts, clients do **not** need an account to browse and review cuts in a shared folder playlist.
:::

---

## 2. Contributor Uploads (Allowing Clients to Upload)

Need a client to send you raw video footage, or an assistant editor to upload their rough cut? You can enable **Contributor Uploads**:

1. In the **Share Folder** window, toggle on **"Allow contributor uploads"**.
2. When visitors open the shared folder link, they will see an **"Upload to this folder"** button.

### How We Protect Your Account Quota
To ensure no random bot or malicious user abuses your storage:
- **Authentication Required for Uploaders**: While viewing is anonymous, uploading requires the contributor to sign in (via Google or Email Magic Link).
- **Strict Guardrails**:
  - Max file size for contributor uploads: **500 MB per file**.
  - Rate limit: **Max 10 uploads per day** per contributor.
  - Quota cap: Contributor uploads cannot exceed **25%** of your total plan quota.
- **Moderation**: You receive an email alert whenever someone uploads to your folder. You can delete the file or revoke the contributor's link at any time.
