Your CrewCut **Library** is where all your video cuts live. It is designed to be clean, distraction-free, and easy to navigate whether you are on a desktop workstation or checking on your phone.

---

## Organizing with Single-Level Folders

To keep things straightforward, CrewCut uses **flat, single-level folders**. You create folders for clients or projects, without confusing nested folders inside folders.

### Creating a Folder
1. Click the **+ New Folder** button on the top left of your Library.
2. Enter a name (for example, `Nike Commercial` or `Dr. Mehta Podcast`).
3. Hit **Enter** or click **Create**.

### Moving Videos into Folders
- **On Desktop**: Drag and drop any video card straight onto the folder in your sidebar or grid.
- **On Mobile / Touchscreen**: Tap the **three dots (⋮)** menu on any video card, select **Move to...**, and pick your folder.

---

## Library Views: Grid vs. List

You can switch views using the toggle icon in the top toolbar:
- **Grid View (Default)**: Visual video cards with big preview thumbnails, file sizes, and time durations. Perfect for visual scanning.
- **List View**: Compact rows showing video title, date created, duration, and status. Great if you manage dozens of cuts at once.

---

## Searching and Sorting

- **Search Bar**: Start typing any part of a video title to filter your library instantly.
- **Sorting Options**: Sort by **Newest First**, **Oldest First**, **Alphabetical (A–Z)**, or **Largest File Size**.

:::tip[Freeing Up Storage]
When a project is wrapped and approved by your client, you can delete old intermediate review cuts to immediately recover your storage quota. Deleted files are permanently removed from the video CDN.
:::
