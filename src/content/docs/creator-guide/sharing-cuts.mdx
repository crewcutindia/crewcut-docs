Sharing a cut in CrewCut is built to remove all friction. There are no permission requests, no client password resets, and no *"I can't open this link"* messages.

---

## How to Copy a Review Link

1. Hover over any video card in your library and click **Share cut** (or open the video and click the **Share** button in the top right).
2. Click **Copy Link**.
3. A private link like `https://app.crewcut.in/review/v/abc123xyz` is copied to your clipboard.

Anyone with this link can watch the video and leave comments immediately.

---

## Download Settings

When you click **Share**, you will see a toggle for **"Allow file download"**:

- **Off (Default)**: Clients can stream and comment on the video, but no download button is shown.
- **On**: A download button appears on the review page, allowing the client to download the original exported video file.

:::tip[Protecting Drafts]
Keep downloads turned **off** for early drafts so clients don't mistakenly post an unapproved or watermarked edit to social media. Turn it on when delivering the final approved master!
:::

---

## Link Security & Expiration

Each review link is cryptographically signed. Video streams are delivered through time-limited CDN tokens:
- Direct video URLs cannot be scraped or hotlinked.
- If you delete a video from your library, the review link is invalidated immediately.
- If your account hits its monthly delivery bandwidth limit, public review links pause automatically to protect you from surprise charges.
