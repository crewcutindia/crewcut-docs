When a client watches your review link and submits feedback, their comments appear in real time in your comment feed.

---

## The Comments Sidebar

Open any video in your authenticated CrewCut library to see the **Feedback Panel** on the right side of the video player:

1. **Exact Timecode Tag**: Every comment shows the exact second it was placed (e.g., `00:32` or `01:45`).
2. **Jump to Frame**: Click any timestamp badge to instantly jump the video player to that exact moment.
3. **Comment Markers on the Timeline**: Small dots appear along the video timeline scrub bar. Hovering over a dot shows a quick preview of that comment.

---

## Replying to Comments

Communication stays clean and organized:
1. Click **Reply** beneath any client comment.
2. Type your response (for example: *"Fixed in the next export!* or *"The audio here is raw; sound mix happens tomorrow"*).
3. Reviewers viewing the link will see your reply threaded under their original note.

---

## Resolving Completed Tasks

As you make edits in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, you can check items off your list:
1. Hover over a comment and click the **Checkmark (Resolve)** button.
2. The comment is dimmed or tucked into the "Resolved" tab.
3. This keeps your active feedback panel focused on what still needs your attention.

:::tip[Working with Multiple Clients]
If multiple people from a client's team leave feedback, their notes all show up chronologically along the timeline with their chosen display names.
:::
