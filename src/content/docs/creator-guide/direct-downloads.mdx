CrewCut features an edge-streaming download architecture that pipes master video files and transcoded proxy cuts directly into your browser's native download manager at maximum connection speed.

---

## 1. How Video Downloads Work

Unlike traditional web tools that force the browser to buffer an entire multi-gigabyte video into memory before prompting you to save, CrewCut uses a streaming Service Worker pipeline:

- **Immediate Save Dialog:** As soon as you click **Download**, your browser prompts you with the standard Save File dialog.
- **Native Browser Download:** The download streams directly into your browser's download manager, showing accurate transfer speed, downloaded bytes, and remaining time.
- **Zero Memory Bottleneck:** You can download 10 GB+ master 4K cuts without your browser tab freezing or crashing.

---

## 2. Choosing Download Resolutions

When you or your reviewers click the download button, CrewCut provides resolution options based on your workflow:

- **Original Master Cut:** The pristine mezzanine video file uploaded by the creator (retains full bitrate, HDR/Dolby Vision metadata, and multi-channel audio).
- **Proxy Rendition (720p / 1080p):** A compressed, lightweight MP4 file ideal for rapid sharing, client previews on low-bandwidth connections, or offline offline mobile viewing.

---

## 3. Controlling Download Permissions on Review Links

As the project owner, you have full control over who can download your files:

1. Open your video or folder in [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Click **Share Link** to open review settings.
3. Toggle **"Allow Video Downloads"**:
   - **Enabled:** Reviewers will see a download button in the player bar and can download the original or proxy cuts.
   - **Disabled:** The download button is hidden, and download API endpoints are locked for that link, restricting viewers to streaming playback only.

---

## 4. Fair-Use Download Rate Limits

To protect video creators against bandwidth abuse and bot scraping, download requests are protected by automated fair-use rate limiting:

- **Rate Limit Window:** Up to 5 download requests per minute per IP address.
- **Active Concurrency:** Maximum of 2 concurrent high-bandwidth download streams per session.
- **Signed Tokens:** All download links use cryptographically signed, expiring URLs that prevent unauthorized hotlinking.
