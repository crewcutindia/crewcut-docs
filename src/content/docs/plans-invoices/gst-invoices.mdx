---

## Automatic PDF Invoices

The moment a payment is confirmed via Razorpay:
1. Our billing system generates a formal **PDF invoice**.
2. A copy is instantly emailed to your registered account email via Resend (`notifications@notification.crewcut.in`).
3. The invoice is permanently archived in your **Billing** tab for on-demand downloads.

---

## Sequential Numbering Standard

To adhere to statutory accounting rules, every invoice carries an immutable, gap-free sequential serial number:
```
CC / [Financial Year] / [Sequence]
Example: CC/2026-27/0042
```

---

## How to Download Past Invoices

1. Sign in to [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Go to **Billing & Invoices**.
3. Scroll down to the **Invoice History** table.
4. Click the **Download PDF** icon next to any transaction.

:::note[Statutory Retention Policy]
Even if you choose to close or delete your CrewCut account in the future, your invoice financial records are securely archived in accordance with Indian statutory accounting and audit retention regulations.
:::
