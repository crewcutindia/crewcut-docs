Whether you are filing business operating expenses or preparing accounts for your chartered accountant, CrewCut provides professional receipts and invoices for every subscription and add-on purchase.

---

## 1. Automatic PDF Invoices

The moment a transaction is confirmed via Razorpay:
1. Our billing system generates an official **PDF Invoice / Receipt**.
2. A copy is sent to your registered account email via Resend (`notifications@notification.crewcut.in`).
3. The invoice is archived permanently in your **Settings** &rarr; **Billing** dashboard for instant download.

---

## 2. Sequential Numbering Standard

To adhere to statutory accounting and audit rules, every invoice carries an immutable, sequential serial number:
```
CC / [Financial Year] / [Sequence Number]
Example: CC/2026-27/0042
```

---

## 3. Tax Treatment (Section 22 CGST Act)

CrewCut currently operates within the statutory threshold exemption under Section 22 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017. Subscription fees and add-on charges displayed on checkout are gross final charges. Invoices reflect valid business expense receipts for income tax and accounting purposes.

---

## 4. Downloading Invoices

1. Sign in to [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Billing**.
3. Under **Invoice History**, click the **Download PDF** button next to any transaction.

:::note[Statutory Retention Policy]
In compliance with Indian commercial and tax recordkeeping laws, invoice records are retained securely for seven (7) years, even if an account is voluntarily closed or deleted.
:::
