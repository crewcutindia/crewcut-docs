## Which Plan Should You Choose?

### 1. Free Tier (2 GB)
Best for: Trying out CrewCut on your current client draft. You can upload 1–2 short cuts, send the link, and see how easy client feedback becomes.

### 2. Starter — ₹199 (10 GB)
Best for: Solo freelance editors handling 2–4 ongoing client video projects per month (social reels, YouTube edits, corporate promo cuts).

### 3. Plus — ₹499 (30 GB)
Best for: Busy commercial creators delivering multi-cam edits, long-form podcast series, and weekly brand deliverables.

### 4. Studio — ₹999 (60 GB)
Best for: Boutique production companies and high-volume post-production studios managing extensive client libraries.
