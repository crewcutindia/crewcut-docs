## Which Plan Fits Your Workflow?

### 1. Free Tier (5 GB)
Best for: Testing CrewCut on your current client draft. Review 2–3 active social cuts or client revisions with full voice notes, frame drawings, and zero-login review links.

### 2. Starter — ₹199 / mo (25 GB)
Best for: Freelance editors delivering weekly social media reels, YouTube cuts, and short corporate promo edits.

### 3. Plus — ₹499 / mo (75 GB)
Best for: Commercial videographers and commercial editors handling multi-cam footage, podcast series, and companion audio stems (`.wav`) with permanent cloud retention.

### 4. Studio — ₹999 / mo (150 GB)
Best for: Creative agencies and boutique post-production houses managing extensive client libraries, 4K/8K master files, and heavy project asset turnover.
