CrewCut partners with **Razorpay Software Private Limited** to process all transactions on PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure across India.

---

## 1. Supported Payment Methods

When checking out on CrewCut, you can pay via:
- **UPI & UPI Autopay:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and bank UPI apps.
- **Credit & Debit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express.
- **NetBanking:** Over 50 Indian scheduled private and public banks.

CrewCut never collects, transmits, or stores credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs on its servers.

---

## 2. Subscriptions & Billing Cycles

- **Monthly Billing:** Subscriptions are billed in advance every 30 days.
- **Auto-Renewal & Control:** If you set up UPI Autopay or a card mandate, your subscription renews automatically so your client review links remain uninterrupted.
- **Cancel Anytime:** You can cancel auto-renewal at any time with a single click in **Settings** &rarr; **Billing**. When cancelled, you retain full tier benefits until the end of your current prepaid period.

---

## 3. Storage Add-on Packs (Non-Recurring)

Unlike base monthly subscriptions, **Storage Add-on Packs** (Seed, Lite, Basic, Standard) are one-time 30-day purchases. They do not auto-renew and automatically lapse after 30 days unless manually renewed.

---

## 4. Refund Policy Summary

Because cloud storage allocations and high-speed transcoding resources are provisioned immediately upon transaction confirmation, paid subscriptions and add-on packs are **non-refundable**.

Full refunds are granted exclusively for:
1. Technical errors resulting in duplicate charges for the same billing period.
2. Verified unauthorized or fraudulent transactions reported to support within 7 calendar days.

For complete terms, review our [Payments, Cancellation & Refunds Policy](https://legal.crewcut.in/paymentsandrefunds).
