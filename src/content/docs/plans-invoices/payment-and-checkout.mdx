CrewCut uses **Razorpay** to process all payments securely across India.

---

## Supported Payment Methods

When checking out through Razorpay, you can pay via:
- **UPI** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred)
- **Credit & Debit Cards** (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex)
- **Netbanking** (All major Indian private and PSU banks)
- **Wallets** (Paytm Wallet, Mobikwik, etc.)

---

## One-Time Orders (No Surprise Auto-Debits)

To respect your financial control:
- In CrewCut, checkout creates a **one-time 30-day order**. 
- We do not mandate hidden recurring subscriptions or auto-charge your credit card unexpectedly.
- When your 30-day period is coming to a close, your billing dashboard will show a friendly reminder:
  > *"Renew before [Date] to keep your plan active."*

:::tip[What Happens if You Don't Renew on Time?]
If your plan expires, your videos are never deleted right away. Your account simply reverts to the Free tier limits. To restore active review links and higher storage, you can renew your plan at any time with a single click.
:::

---

## Upgrading Mid-Cycle

If you start on the Starter tier (₹199) and realize you need more storage for a big commercial shoot, you can upgrade directly to Plus (₹499) or Studio (₹999) from your **Billing** tab. Your storage quota expands immediately upon payment confirmation.
