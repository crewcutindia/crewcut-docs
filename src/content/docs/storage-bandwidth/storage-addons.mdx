When working against tight client delivery deadlines or managing multiple 4K commercial cuts at once, you might need immediate storage headroom without switching your monthly subscription tier.

CrewCut provides instant, one-click **Storage Add-on Packs** that expand your capacity for 30 days.

---

## 1. Storage Add-on Options & Pricing

Add-on packs are one-time purchases denominated in Indian Rupees (INR &#8377;):

| Pack Name | Extra Storage Allocation | Price (INR) | Validity Period | Renewal Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seed Pack** | **+10 GB** | &#8377;59 | 30 Days | Manual (One-time) |
| **Lite Pack** | **+25 GB** | &#8377;139 | 30 Days | Manual (One-time) |
| **Basic Pack** | **+30 GB** | &#8377;159 | 30 Days | Manual (One-time) |
| **Standard Pack** | **+50 GB** | &#8377;249 | 30 Days | Manual (One-time) |

---

## 2. Key Rules & Behavior

- **Instant Provisioning:** Your storage quota increases the moment Razorpay confirms payment. No waiting for manual support approvals.
- **Stackable:** You can purchase multiple add-on packs if needed. Their capacities stack on top of your base plan.
- **No Recurring Autopay:** Storage add-on packs **do not auto-renew**. They remain active for exactly 30 days from purchase.
- **Unified Usage:** Add-on storage can be used for original master video cuts, transcoded HLS streams, or companion assets (`.wav`, `.psd`, `.prproj`).
- **Post-Expiry:** After 30 days, your account returns to its base plan quota. Make sure to delete delivered cuts or renew an add-on before expiration to prevent upload locking.

---

## 3. How to Purchase an Add-on Pack

1. Sign in to your dashboard at [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Billing**.
3. Under **Storage Add-ons**, choose the pack that fits your current project load.
4. Complete payment securely via Razorpay (UPI, Card, or NetBanking). Your new storage limit is reflected immediately.
