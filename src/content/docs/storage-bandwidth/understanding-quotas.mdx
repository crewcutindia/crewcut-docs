To keep CrewCut ultra-fast, affordable, and transparent, every plan includes **Cloud Storage Capacity**, **Data Retention Guarantees**, and **Monthly Delivery Bandwidth**.

---

## 1. Cloud Storage Capacity (GB Stored)

This is the total storage your active video cuts, transcoded streams, and companion assets occupy across your library:

- **Adaptive Video Renditions:** When you upload a video cut, our edge video engine transcodes multiple adaptive HLS resolutions (from 360p up to 4K / 8K Dolby Vision) so reviewers on any network or device enjoy stutter-free playback. Multi-resolution streaming renditions and mezzanine masters are accounted for in your storage meter.
- **Companion Assets (CrewCut Drive):** Production files uploaded alongside your cuts—such as `.wav` audio stems, `.psd` graphics, or `.prproj`/`.aep` project files—count towards your unified storage allocation.
- **Instant Space Recovery:** When you delete a completed or outdated video or folder from your library, all associated renditions and files are purged immediately, freeing up your quota instantly.

---

## 2. Retention Guarantees

Depending on your plan tier, inactive video cuts are governed by retention windows:

- **Free Tier (7-Day Retention):** Inactive review cuts that have not received comments or views for 7 calendar days are scheduled for cleanup.
- **Starter Tier (14-Day Retention):** Inactive review cuts are retained for 14 calendar days.
- **Plus & Studio Tiers (Permanent Retention):** All video cuts, companion assets, and review comments are retained permanently for as long as your subscription is active.
