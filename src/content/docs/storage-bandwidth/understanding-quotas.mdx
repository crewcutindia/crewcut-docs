To keep CrewCut fast, affordable, and transparent, every plan includes two separate quotas: **Stored Video Capacity** and **Monthly Delivery Bandwidth**.

---

## 1. Stored Video Capacity (GB Stored)

This is the total disk space your active videos occupy in your library.

### The Bunny Stream Encoding Multiplier (~1.5×)
When you upload a master video (for example, a 1 GB MP4 file), our video engine (Bunny Stream) encodes multiple web-friendly versions (360p, 720p, 1080p, and HLS streaming playlists) so that clients on slow mobile networks or fast broadband can both stream smoothly.

Because of these extra streaming renditions:
- Total stored bytes in your account are roughly **$\sim 1.5\times$** the raw file you uploaded.
- For example, an uploaded 1 GB file takes about **1.5 GB** of stored space in your account.

:::tip[Freeing Space Instantly]
Whenever you delete an old or approved video from your Library, all its stored resolutions are immediately purged from the CDN, recovering your storage quota instantly.
:::

---

## 2. Monthly Delivery Bandwidth (GB Streamed)

Delivery bandwidth measures the data transferred over the global CDN when reviewers watch your cuts.

- **Pro-rated Watch Time**: We only count bandwidth for the portions of the video your clients actually watch. If you send a 10-minute video (500 MB) and the client only watches 2 minutes, only $\sim 100$ MB of delivery bandwidth is consumed.
- **Monthly Reset**: Your delivery bandwidth counter resets automatically on the first day of every billing cycle.
