If you run a commercial production house, ad agency, or high-volume editing studio handling dozens of 4K deliverables every week, the standard **Studio (60 GB)** tier might not be enough.

You can request custom high-capacity storage tailored to your team.

---

## How to Request Custom Storage

1. Go to your **Billing** tab in [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in).
2. Scroll to the **Custom Capacity** section and click **Request Storage Quote**.
3. Select your desired storage size (e.g., **150 GB**, **300 GB**, or **1 TB+**).
4. Submit your request.

Our team will review your bandwidth profile and send you a custom invoice quote via email. Once settled via Razorpay, your account quota is upgraded within hours with a custom `quota_override_gb` allocation.

---

## What is Included in Custom Storage?

- Custom storage capacity (up to multiple terabytes).
- Proportionately increased monthly CDN delivery bandwidth.
- Dedicated support for direct studio migrations.
