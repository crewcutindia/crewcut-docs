Most cloud video providers use "pay-as-you-go" bandwidth billing. If an agency shares a cut that gets sent around to 200 people, the creator gets hit with an unexpected \$100+ bill at the end of the month.

**CrewCut is built differently.** We protect you from surprise charges.

---

## The Delivery Cap Safety Guard

Each plan comes with a hard monthly delivery bandwidth limit (for example, **20 GB/month** on Starter or **60 GB/month** on Plus).

If your shared review links hit 100% of your monthly delivery limit:
1. **Zero Overcharge**: You will **never** be billed surprise overage fees.
2. **Review Link Pause (HTTP 429)**: Instead of incurring unlimited debt, public review links pause and display a polite notice:
   > *"This creator's monthly delivery bandwidth limit has been reached. Please contact the creator to resume viewing."*
3. **Your Videos Remain Safe**: Your videos in your creator library are never touched or deleted. You can still access them, download them, or upgrade your plan to immediately unblock public review links.

---

## How to Check Your Current Delivery Usage

1. Open [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in) and navigate to **Billing & Usage**.
2. Look at the **Delivery Bandwidth Meter**. It shows exactly how many gigabytes have been delivered this month.
3. You can click the **Sync Usage** button anytime to fetch the latest pro-rated CDN numbers immediately.

:::tip[How to Avoid Hitting Delivery Caps]
- Keep draft cuts compressed to 1080p rather than uncompressed 4K.
- Delete or archive review links for client projects that have already been finalized.
- Upgrade to the next plan if you are consistently sending cuts to large client teams.
:::
