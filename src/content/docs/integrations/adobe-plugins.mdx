Stop switching back and forth between your web browser and your editing timeline. The official **CrewCut Adobe Extension** brings client feedback, frame-accurate notes, and reviewer comments directly into your Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects sequences.

---

## 1. Extension Capabilities

- **Native Timeline Markers:** Converts client comments into native timeline sequence markers with exact timecode alignment.
- **Marker Color Coding:** Unresolved feedback appears as yellow markers; approved/resolved notes turn green.
- **Comment Metadata:** Double-clicking any marker reveals the reviewer's name, timestamp, comment text, and a direct link to listen to their voice note.
- **Two-Way Sync:** Mark comments as resolved directly inside Premiere Pro or After Effects, and CrewCut instantly updates the review link for your client.

---

## 2. Installation & Setup

### Step 1: Download the Extension
Download the latest `CrewCut-Adobe-v1.0.zxp` package from your [CrewCut Account Settings](https://app.crewcut.in/settings/plugins).

### Step 2: Install via Extension Manager
You can install the `.zxp` file using any standard extension installer:
1. Open **Anastasiy’s Extension Manager** or **ZXP Installer** (both free for Windows & macOS).
2. Drag and drop `CrewCut-Adobe-v1.0.zxp` into the manager window.
3. Restart Adobe Premiere Pro or After Effects.

### Step 3: Open the Panel
1. Inside Premiere Pro or After Effects, navigate to the top menu bar:
   `Window` &rarr; `Extensions` &rarr; `CrewCut Review`.
2. Dock the CrewCut panel anywhere in your workspace (such as next to the Effect Controls or Project panel).

---

## 3. Connecting to Your Workspace

1. In the extension panel, click **Sign In**.
2. A secure browser window opens in [app.crewcut.in](https://app.crewcut.in) to generate your plugin access token.
3. Click **Authorize Extension**. Your project folders and video cuts will immediately populate inside the Adobe panel.

---

## 4. Synchronizing Markers with Your Active Sequence

1. Open your timeline sequence in Premiere Pro or composition in After Effects.
2. In the CrewCut panel, select the video cut that corresponds to your timeline.
3. Click **Sync Markers to Sequence**:
   - The extension reads the timecode offset of your sequence.
   - All reviewer comments are created as sequence markers at the exact corresponding frames.
4. Use standard Premiere shortcuts (`Shift + M` to jump to the next marker; `Ctrl + Shift + M` to go to the previous marker) to fly through client revisions.

---

## 5. Resolving Feedback

As you make each requested cut:
1. In the CrewCut extension panel, check the box next to the comment to mark it as **Resolved**.
2. The sequence marker turns green on your timeline.
3. Your client's review link updates in real time, letting them know revision notes have been addressed.
