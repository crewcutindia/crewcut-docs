No more writing vague notes like *"at around the 2-minute mark"*. CrewCut automatically synchronizes your notes with the video timeline.

---

## 1. Automatic Timestamping

When you start typing in the comment box, CrewCut automatically attaches the current playhead position (for example, `01:14`).

- If you want to leave a general note about the whole video (like *"Overall tone is awesome, just need color correction"*), you can uncheck the timestamp box.
- Otherwise, leave it checked so your editor knows the exact frame you are pointing at.

---

## 2. Setting Your Display Name

The first time you post a comment:
1. CrewCut asks you for your name (e.g. *"Priya (Client)"* or *"Rahul - Marketing"*).
2. That name is saved for your session so your editor knows who left which note.
3. No password or email verification is required to leave comments.

---

## 3. Scrubbing to Existing Notes

On the right side of the video player, you will see the **Comment Stream**:
- Each card shows the author, their message, and a timestamp pill like `00:45`.
- **Click any timestamp pill** to instantly jump the video player to that exact moment so you can see what someone else was talking about.
- You can reply directly to any comment to start a discussion thread.
