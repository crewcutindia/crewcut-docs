If your editor or video producer sent you a CrewCut link, you are in the right place! 

Here is everything you need to know in under a minute.

---

## Do I need to create an account?

**No.** You do **not** need to sign up, remember passwords, or download any software. 

CrewCut is designed so you can open the review link on your computer, tablet, or smartphone and review the video right away.

---

## How to Review a Video

1. **Open the link**: Click the link your creator sent you (e.g. on WhatsApp, Slack, or email).
2. **Hit Play**: Watch the video in high definition.
3. **Pause where you want changes**: Whenever you see something you want modified (a typo in a title, awkward cut, audio volume), pause the video.
4. **Type your note**: Enter your feedback in the comment box at the bottom.
5. **Submit**: Hit **Enter** or tap **Post Comment**. 

Your note is instantly stamped with the exact second on the video!

:::tip[Desktop Shortcuts]
- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause
- **Left / Right Arrow**: Skip backward or forward 5 seconds
- **J / K / L**: Standard video scrub shortcuts
:::

---

## Can I review on my phone?

**Yes!** The review page is fully optimized for mobile browsers. You can scrub through the cut on your iPhone or Android phone, tap on timestamp tags, and leave feedback on the go.
