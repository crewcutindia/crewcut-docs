When you leave feedback on a video, you might want to know when your editor replies or addresses your notes.

---

## The "Get Email Updates" Prompt

After you submit your first comment on a review link, a polite prompt will ask:

> **"Want email updates when the editor replies to your feedback?"**

- **If you enter your email**: You will receive a clean notification whenever the creator replies to your comments or resolves your feedback.
- **If you click "Not now"**: The prompt disappears and will not bother you again on that cut.

:::note[Zero Spam Promise]
We only use your email to send notifications directly related to that specific video cut. You will never receive marketing emails, newsletters, or promotional messages.
:::

---

## Checking Back Later

If you didn't subscribe to email alerts, no problem! You can always open the original review link anytime. All comments, creator replies, and resolution badges stay updated in real time.
